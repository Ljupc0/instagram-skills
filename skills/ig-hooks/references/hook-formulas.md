# Hook mechanisms

These are writing patterns, not proven viral formulas. Use only claims supported by the content and brief.

1. Specific problem: If [situation], check [overlooked cause].
2. Demonstration first: Show the outcome, then explain the process.
3. Process change: Explain what changed without inventing results.
4. Common mistake: Name an observable mistake and a practical correction.
5. Decision guide: Choose A under one condition, B under another.
6. Useful constraint: Complete a modest task with a real resource limit.
7. Audience question: Answer an actual recurring question.
8. Evidence-based myth: Challenge a claim with reliable evidence.
9. Checklist: Offer specific checks before a defined decision.
10. Counterexample: Explain when common advice fails and still applies.
11. Behind the scenes: Show a real process without fake exclusivity.
12. Story tension: A documented obstacle, decision and lesson.
13. Comparison: Compare equivalent options with explicit criteria.
14. Teardown: Analyze a supplied example with attribution.
15. Experiment: Show what will be tested without announcing results early.
16. Objection: Address a genuine concern without pressure.
17. Beginner path: Show the smallest useful first step.
18. Diagnostic: Name a symptom and possible explanations, with uncertainty.
19. Tradeoff: Reveal what is gained and sacrificed in a decision.
20. Visual reveal: Reveal a meaningful detail rather than empty suspense.
21. Short tutorial: Show a usable sequence for one task.
22. FAQ contrast: Explain why two similar things differ.
23. Evidence walkthrough: Explain what a real result does and does not show.
24. Resource saving: Demonstrate a specific efficiency without inflated savings.
25. Scenario: Apply advice to a clearly labeled hypothetical situation.
26. Lesson learned: Use the owner's genuine documented experience.

For each hook, verify that the promised payoff appears in the content. Use natural speech and concrete nouns. Avoid fake secrets, guarantees and urgency. Meaningful variants change mechanism or angle, not just wording.
