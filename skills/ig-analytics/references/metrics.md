# Metric definitions and comparisons

Use the export's definitions where they differ. Show formulas and units.

- Reach: unique accounts reached when provided by the platform.
- Views/plays: platform-defined events, not necessarily unique people.
- Interaction rate by reach: interactions / reach × 100. State included actions.
- Save/share rate: saves or shares / reach × 100.
- Follow conversion: attributed follows / matching profile visits × 100, only with compatible attribution.
- Link CTR: link clicks / matching exposure denominator × 100. Name the denominator.
- Average watch percentage: average watch time / duration × 100 where compatible; loops can exceed 100%.
- Completion rate: completed views / eligible starts only when both are provided.
- Relative outlier: post metric / median comparable baseline metric, with sample size and period. Zero baseline makes this undefined.

A missing or zero denominator means rate unavailable, not zero. Aggregate compatible counts before computing a weighted rate; do not average row-level percentages. Do not sum post reach and label it unique account reach. Compare absolute outcomes and rates. Separate paid/organic, formats and exposure ages. Flag small samples and confounders. Correlation does not establish lift or sales attribution.
