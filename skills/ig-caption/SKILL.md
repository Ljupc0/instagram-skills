# Instagram captions and hashtags

Read [references/profile-contract.md](references/profile-contract.md) before this workflow. When creating or repairing a brief, use [references/profile-template.md](references/profile-template.md).

## Workflow

Inspect the asset or script; never claim to have watched unavailable media. Write a useful opening, concise body and one primary CTA aligned with the objective. Match audience language and voice.
Provide a ready-to-use caption and a shorter version when useful. Include only relevant hashtags if requested or helpful; do not invent a required count or promise reach. Verify current field limits when fit matters. Flag known commercial relationships for appropriate disclosure review; verify applicable requirements rather than inventing legal advice. Do not publish.

## Improvement responsibility

Tie the caption to the selected content hypothesis and actual asset. Add specific useful context rather than another vague wealth, fitness or success slogan. Use a CTA that elicits relevant behavior for the goal, not empty engagement. Preserve the chosen language and recurring voice. Mark performance as unmeasured until published evidence exists.
