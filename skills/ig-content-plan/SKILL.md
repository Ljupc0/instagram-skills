# Weekly publishing and connection plan

Read [references/profile-contract.md](references/profile-contract.md) before this workflow. When creating or repairing a brief, use [references/profile-template.md](references/profile-template.md).

## Workflow

Resolve profile, target week, timezone, business goal and realistic capacity. Read strategy and recent findings if available. Build seven dated rows including rest or engagement-only days when appropriate.
Deliver local date/time | format | topic | objective | hook | CTA | assets | preparation deadline | status | success metric. Before choosing a publication time, inspect available audience-active times and their reporting timezone. If unavailable, research publication timestamps from a relevant competitor sample, preserving source URLs, UTC timestamps, formats, date range and local timezone conversions including daylight saving. Distinguish the post timestamp from comment timestamps. Competitor posting frequency by hour is not proof of a best-performing hour; related commercial pages, different content ages, paid posts and different audiences limit inference. Select a justified trial slot and a comparison window rather than inventing an optimum or postponing forever for certainty. Base later changes on the target account's comparable actual results. Check daylight-saving transitions for the actual week.
Add a manageable connection shortlist: verified person/brand and public link, fit, relationship goal, specific interaction or outreach draft and follow-up condition. Separate clients, collaborators and learning peers. Use supplied contacts or current research; never invent contacts or claim contact was made. Use ig-collab-pitch for commercial drafts if available.
Coordinate only the skills necessary for requested deliverables; a plan does not require all scripts or paid assets. Finish with a batch-production checklist and end-of-week review. Do not schedule, post or send outreach without explicit authorization and actual access.

## Improvement responsibility

Read the current diagnosis and experiment backlog. Attach an experiment ID and rationale to substantive planned posts. Include a numeric production-time ledger, an explicit capacity assumption when unknown, and a reduced-scope fallback. Keep immutable character/provider/duration constraints in asset handoffs. A same-day weekly review may be preliminary; derive each final measurement date from its own publication date and exposure window. Reuse stable patterns and change only a few deliberate variables. Do not claim a global best posting time for an international audience with unknown markets.
