# Positioning and content strategy

Read [references/profile-contract.md](references/profile-contract.md) before this workflow. When creating or repairing a brief, use [references/profile-template.md](references/profile-template.md).

## Workflow

Use the brief and available results to identify the priority audience problem, offer, credible difference and objective. Challenge vague positioning and unrealistic output expectations with concrete reasons.
Deliver positioning, evidence-based audience segments, three to five pillars with angles, funnel roles, sustainable format mix and primary metrics. Tie each pillar to an audience need and business purpose. Add an experiment backlog with review criteria rather than guaranteed growth targets.
Separate evidence from assumptions and explain what would change the direction. Hand strategy and capacity constraints to ig-content-plan. Do not invent market demand or choose niches from sensitive personal traits.

## Improvement responsibility

For a broad existing-profile improvement request, use or perform ig-growth-audit and read the latest private experiment log. For a new account without validated direction, use ig-niche-research before choosing a niche. Rank opportunities with evidence and uncertainty; do not merely prescribe content pillars. Map each selected action to a diagnosed bottleneck or labeled hypothesis and the improvement-cycle experiment fields. Preserve existing successful identity and owner production preferences. Produce a small prioritized experiment backlog, not a universal growth recipe.
