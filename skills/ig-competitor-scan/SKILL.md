# Competitor profile comparison

Read [references/profile-contract.md](references/profile-contract.md) before this workflow. When creating or repairing a brief, use [references/profile-template.md](references/profile-template.md).

## Workflow

For a full profile-improvement strategy, research at least five relevant profiles and at least ten actual accessible posts per profile. A user-requested narrow comparison may use fewer, explicitly labeled as a partial comparison. Select by audience, topic and presentation; explain each inclusion and distinguish direct competitors, adjacent craft references and small counterexamples. Do not pad the five with irrelevant accounts. If access prevents the minimum, preserve useful findings but label the strategy provisional and list missing coverage.
Record URLs, observation dates, post dates, selection method, sample windows and sizes. Distinguish grid-caption review, opened post metadata, inspected slides/frames, and fully reviewed media/audio. Analyze actual accessible posts or supplied screenshots; inaccessible links are not evidence. Inspect representative creative sequences as well as captions; never claim every slide or full video was reviewed from one screenshot.
Record bio, positioning, identity, accessible format mix, offers, calls to action and audience questions. Check common ownership or shared commercial funnels so related pages are not presented as independent confirmations. Separate keyword requests for offers from substantive comments, spam and author replies.
Compare positioning, offers, themes, hook mechanisms, formats, cadence and audience questions. Normalize visible performance against each account's comparable typical posts when enough data exists. Do not infer private conversions, saves or profit.
Deliver a source-linked comparison with a post inventory, coverage limits and an adopt/adapt/avoid decision for each profile. Explain the target profile's own audience promise and how each proposed improvement will be tested; combining five popular patterns does not prove a better-performing account. A gap is a hypothesis without audience evidence. Do not copy scripts or imply recurring monitoring is scheduled. Research across broader breakout content belongs to ig-viral-research.

## Improvement responsibility

Include both established references for craft and more comparable accounts for opportunity testing. Large follower totals alone do not prove recent growth. To claim growth require dated observations or owner-supplied history and calculate changes over that interval; otherwise describe only observed current scale/engagement. Check smaller-account relative outliers where baselines are available. Link each transferable mechanism to a profile-specific hypothesis, without copying identity or claiming competitor sales.
