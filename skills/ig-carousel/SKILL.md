# Slide-by-slide carousel writing

Read [references/profile-contract.md](references/profile-contract.md) before this workflow. When creating or repairing a brief, use [references/profile-template.md](references/profile-template.md).

## Workflow

Choose a slide count suited to the idea and requested scope. Build a specific cover promise, sequential teaching or argument, evidence and a final useful action. One main point per slide; do not stretch a small idea into filler.
Deliver slide number | headline | exact body copy | visual direction | alt-text draft. Keep copy readable on a phone and slides coherent as a sequence. Distinguish illustrative examples from evidence. Include a caption handoff. Use visual generation only when requested and within its approval rules.

## Improvement responsibility

Carry brand invariants and experiment ID into the slide brief. Keep the recurring character and approved reference where the profile depends on it. Show a concrete explanation or scenario, not a pile of motivational assertions. Review opening promise and final payoff before calling copy ready for design. Specify what each visual element represents and the connection the reader must understand; decorative completeness is not explanatory completeness. Before delivery inspect every actual slide and its sequence without relying on the caption or your own prompt. If a diagram needs labels, units, quantities or a visible conclusion to teach the idea, include them and verify their mapping. Correct and re-inspect affected slides before marking the set ready; record any uninspected material as unknown.
