# Version 0.2 evaluation record

Date: 2026-11-01. Status: experimental. No demonstrated Instagram growth claim.

## Changes evaluated

Outcome-oriented responsibilities were added to the original 19 skills. Three roles were added: existing-account diagnosis, new-niche validation and production-quality review. Every skill carries the same improvement-cycle reference and preserves owner identity, language, tool, evidence limits and experiment state.

## Actual checks

- Package validation and official Codex structural validation passed for 22 skills.
- A deterministic rebuild produced identical generated skill files.
- Three separate evaluators used the actual updated files on recorded cases, with no live actions or spending. These were single-pass qualitative tests, not statistical reliability estimates or automatic discovery tests.
- Brand case: owner selected a recurring mascot, English, Google Flow and 8–10 seconds; original reference and new spending approval absent. Output retained these choices, rejected a generic slogan, supplied a concrete concept and kept production blocked on explicit dependencies. Finished media remained partial, not falsely passed.
- Niche case: owner had bicycle-maintenance knowledge, limited time and a huge-account screenshot for an unrelated investing niche. Output recommended a bounded owner-fit test without claiming verified growth or market proof. An issue was observed: early concepts favored easy topics over the strongest supplied audience questions. The skill was revised to make this tradeoff explicit and require relevant demand-linked concepts or a specific competence check. In the second pass, concepts addressed the strongest question category with an explicitly narrower, competence-appropriate angle; the evaluator correctly noted that this does not validate demand for a repair tutorial. This is a better bounded test, not proof of a winning niche.
- Measurement case: old photo 10 saves/100 reach at day 7; new Reel 12/200 at day 1. Output calculated 10% and 6%, retained incompatible age/format/topic limits and concluded inconclusive. A November 6 post's seven-day review was correctly moved to November 13, not November 8. Nothing was scheduled.

## Limits

Only targeted revised responsibilities were behaviorally evaluated in these three cases; the remaining revised skills passed structural checks but were not all re-executed independently. Prior v1 test outputs are historical and do not validate v2 wholesale. Live automatic discovery, production fidelity, private inbox operations, publication, paid generation and real growth remain separate validation tasks.

For a real profile, preserve baseline and failed attempts locally, inspect a relevant benchmark set, approve a small production pilot, then measure comparable published observations. A profile-specific diagnosis or attractive draft is not a success story. Do not advertise flawless operation or growth results from this record.
